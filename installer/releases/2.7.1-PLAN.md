No outside review of 2.7.0 has arrived yet (the Gemini brief is in `Desktop\DeskTrace updates`). 2.7.1 therefore closes what
2.7.0 itself left weak — the limits written in the 2.7.0 plan and brief, plus a self-review of the new window code, which
had no automated test (the screenshot runs found two window bugs). Same fixed rules as 2.7.0 (4 entry points, local, no new
runtime dependencies, en + zh, old data readable, nothing destructive on its own, never test on the owner's real data).
